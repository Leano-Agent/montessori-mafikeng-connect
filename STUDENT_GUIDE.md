## 🎯 What You're Learning

### Montessori Areas Made Simple

#### 1. Practical Life
**You learn:** How to take care of yourself and your environment
**Examples:** Pouring water, buttoning, cleaning, gardening
**Look for:** "I can do it myself!" moments

#### 2. Sensorial
**You learn:** Using all your senses to explore
**Examples:** Feeling textures, smelling spices, matching colors
**Look for:** "This feels/smells/looks interesting!"

#### 3. Language
**You learn:** Reading, writing, and communicating
**Examples:** Sandpaper letters, movable alphabet, stories
**Look for:** "I can read/write/tell this!"

#### 4. Mathematics
**You learn:** Numbers, patterns, and problem-solving
**Examples:** Number rods, golden beads, multiplication board
**Look for:** "I figured it out!" moments

#### 5. Cultural
**You learn:** About the world and different cultures
**Examples:** Maps, landforms, African artifacts, science experiments
**Look for:** "I wonder about..." questions

## 🎯 Setting Learning Goals

### Simple Goal Setting

#### For Younger Students:
**Example Goals:**
- "I want to learn to tie my shoes"
- "I want to read 5 new books"
- "I want to help water plants every day"
- "I want to make a new friend"

**How to Set:**
1. Think: What do I want to learn?
2. Tell: Your teacher or parent
3. Write: Simple sentence (with help)
4. Try: Work on it little by little
5. Celebrate: When you reach your goal!

#### For Older Students:
**SMART Goals:**
- **Specific**: Exactly what you want to learn
- **Measurable**: How you'll know you did it
- **Achievable**: Something you can really do
- **Relevant**: Important for your learning
- **Timely**: When you want to finish by

**Example SMART Goal:**
"I will learn to multiply numbers up to 10x10 by practicing 10 minutes daily for 2 weeks, and I'll show my teacher I can do it by solving 20 problems correctly."

### Tracking Your Goals
1. **Check-in**: Look at your goals each week
2. **Update**: Mark progress as you go
3. **Adjust**: Change if needed (that's okay!)
4. **Complete**: Celebrate when finished!
5. **New Goal**: Set your next challenge

## 🛡️ Staying Safe Online

### Important Rules

#### Personal Information:
- ❌ Never share your full name online
- ❌ Never share your address or phone number
- ❌ Never share your school name
- ✅ Only use your first name or nickname
- ✅ Ask before sharing any information

#### Photos and Videos:
- ❌ Never post photos without permission
- ❌ Never share photos of other children
- ❌ Never take photos in private spaces
- ✅ Only share school work photos
- ✅ Always ask teacher or parent first

#### Communication:
- ❌ Never talk to strangers online
- ❌ Never share passwords
- ❌ Never meet anyone from online
- ✅ Only message people you know
- ✅ Tell an adult if something feels wrong

### What to Do If...
- **You see something scary**: Tell a teacher or parent immediately
- **Someone asks for personal information**: Say no and tell an adult
- **You feel uncomfortable**: Close the app and get help
- **You make a mistake**: It's okay! Tell an adult and learn from it

## ❓ Questions & Help

### Who to Ask for Help

#### At School:
- Your Montessori teacher
- Classroom assistant
- School technology helper
- Principal or administrator

#### At Home:
- Your parents or caregivers
- Older siblings (with parent permission)
- Family friends who know about technology

### How to Ask for Help
1. **Be specific**: "I can't find my photos" not "It's broken"
2. **Show the problem**: Point to what's not working
3. **Be patient**: Sometimes technology needs time
4. **Say thank you**: Always appreciate help

### Common Problems & Solutions

#### "I forgot my password"
- Tell your teacher
- They will help reset it
- Create a new one you can remember
- Write it down in a safe place (with help)

#### "The app won't open"
- Check if device has internet
- Close and reopen the app
- Tell your teacher if it keeps happening
- Use the website instead if needed

#### "I can't find my work"
- Ask teacher to help search
- Check different sections
- Look by date or subject
- It might be loading slowly

#### "I made a mistake"
- It's okay! Everyone makes mistakes
- Tell your teacher what happened
- They can help fix it
- Learn for next time
