# Montessori Mafikeng Connect - Code of Conduct

## 🌍 Our Commitment

At Montessori Mafikeng Connect, we are committed to creating a respectful, inclusive, and supportive environment for all members of our educational community. This Code of Conduct applies to all users of our platform: administrators, teachers, parents, students, and any other participants.

## 🦁 African Values We Uphold

### Ubuntu Philosophy
We believe in "I am because we are" - our humanity is interconnected. We strive to:
- Treat everyone with dignity and respect
- Support each other's growth and development
- Build community through collaboration
- Honor our shared African heritage

### Montessori Principles
- Respect for the child as an individual
- Peaceful conflict resolution
- Grace and courtesy in all interactions
- Support for each person's unique developmental journey

## 📜 Expected Behavior

### For All Community Members
1. **Respectful Communication**
   - Use kind and constructive language
   - Listen actively to others
   - Express disagreements respectfully
   - Avoid offensive or discriminatory language

2. **Cultural Sensitivity**
   - Respect diverse backgrounds and perspectives
   - Use appropriate language and terminology
   - Celebrate cultural differences
   - Avoid stereotypes and assumptions

3. **Professional Boundaries**
   - Maintain appropriate teacher-student relationships
   - Respect privacy and confidentiality
   - Use platform features appropriately
   - Report concerns through proper channels

4. **Digital Citizenship**
   - Use technology responsibly
   - Protect personal and others' information
   - Respect intellectual property
   - Report technical issues appropriately

### Specific Guidelines by Role

#### For Administrators:
- Lead by example in all interactions
- Ensure fair and equitable treatment
- Protect sensitive information
- Respond promptly to concerns
- Support professional development

#### For Teachers:
- Maintain professional educator standards
- Document observations objectively
- Communicate with parents respectfully
- Protect student privacy and dignity
- Model Montessori values daily

#### For Parents:
- Partner respectfully with educators
- Support your child's learning journey
- Communicate concerns constructively
- Respect school policies and procedures
- Engage positively with school community

#### For Students (age-appropriate):
- Be kind to classmates and teachers
- Use technology responsibly
- Respect others' work and space
- Ask for help when needed
- Follow school rules online and offline

## 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

### Zero Tolerance Behaviors
The following behaviors will not be tolerated and may result in immediate account suspension or termination:

1. **Harassment or Bullying**
   - Targeted intimidation or threats
   - Repeated unwanted contact
   - Public or private harassment
   - Cyberbullying in any form

2. **Discrimination**
   - Racist, sexist, homophobic, or ableist language
   - Discrimination based on any protected characteristic
   - Exclusionary behavior or remarks
   - Cultural insensitivity or appropriation

3. **Privacy Violations**
   - Sharing others' personal information without consent
   - Unauthorized photo or video sharing
   - Accessing others' accounts or data
   - Recording conversations without permission

4. **Professional Misconduct**
   - Inappropriate relationships with students
   - Breach of confidentiality
   - Misuse of platform or data
   - Failure to report safety concerns

5. **Platform Abuse**
   - Spamming or flooding communications
   - Creating fake accounts
   - Attempting to hack or disrupt service
   - Sharing inappropriate content

## ⚖️ Reporting & Enforcement

### How to Report Concerns

#### Immediate Safety Concerns
- **Child safety issues**: Contact school administration immediately
- **Emergency situations**: Use emergency contact numbers
- **Platform security breaches**: Report to technical support

#### General Concerns
1. **In-App Reporting**: Use "Report" feature on concerning content
2. **Email**: conduct@montessori-mafikeng.connect
3. **School Channels**: Report to appropriate school personnel
4. **Anonymous Reporting**: Available through school administration

### What to Include in Reports
- Specific description of incident
- Date, time, and location (digital or physical)
- Names of people involved
- Screenshots or evidence (if digital)
- Any witnesses
- Desired outcome or resolution

### Investigation Process
1. **Initial Review**: Within 24 hours of report
2. **Confidential Investigation**: By designated personnel
3. **Stakeholder Interviews**: Gather all perspectives
4. **Evidence Review**: Examine all available information
5. **Decision Making**: Based on facts and policies
6. **Communication**: Outcome shared with relevant parties
7. **Appeal Process**: Available for significant decisions

### Consequences
Depending on severity and circumstances, consequences may include:

1. **Warning**: Formal notice for minor first offenses
2. **Temporary Suspension**: Limited platform access
3. **Mandatory Training**: Required completion of relevant courses
4. **Account Restrictions**: Limited functionality
5. **Permanent Ban**: Complete removal from platform
6. **Legal Action**: For serious violations or illegal activities

- Respect for individual privacy rights

### User Responsibilities
- Protect your login credentials
- Report suspected security breaches

- Explicit consent required for all student photos

- Teachers document only with permission
- Media used only for educational purposes

## 🌐 Online Community Guidelines

### Communication Standards
1. **Professional Tone**
   - Use proper grammar and spelling
   - Avoid slang or informal language in official communications
   - Be clear and concise

2. **Cultural Considerations**
   - Use appropriate greetings and titles
   - Respect cultural communication styles
   - Be mindful of language preferences
   - Avoid assumptions based on culture

3. **Conflict Resolution**
   - Address concerns privately first
   - Focus on issues, not personalities
   - Seek mediation when needed
   - Follow established escalation paths

### Social Media & External Sharing
- Do not share confidential school information
- Respect others' privacy in shared content
- Credit sources appropriately
- Represent the school community positively
- Separate personal and professional accounts
