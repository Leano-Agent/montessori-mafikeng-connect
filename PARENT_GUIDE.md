# Montessori Mafikeng Connect - Parent Guide

## 👋 Dumela! Welcome Parents!

This guide is specifically for parents using Montessori Mafikeng Connect. It will help you stay connected with your child's Montessori education, track their progress, and communicate effectively with teachers.

## 🚀 Quick Start for Parents

### Step 1: Get Access
1. **Receive Invitation**: Check your email for invitation from school
2. **Create Account**: Click link and set up password
3. **Verify Email**: Confirm your email address
4. **Add Children**: Enter your child's information using school-provided code

### Step 2: Install Mobile App
1. Open `https://montessori-mafikeng.connect` on your phone
2. Tap "Share" → "Add to Home Screen"
3. Name it "Montessori Connect"
4. Now you have an app icon on your home screen!

### Step 3: First Login
1. Open the app
2. Enter email and password
3. Choose language (Setswana or English)
4. Explore your dashboard

## 📊 Understanding Montessori Progress

### Montessori Areas Explained

#### 1. Practical Life
- Self-care skills (dressing, washing)
- Care of environment (cleaning, gardening)
- Grace and courtesy (manners, social skills)
- Movement and coordination

#### 2. Sensorial
- Exploring senses (touch, smell, sight)
- Sorting and classifying
- Understanding dimensions
- Color and shape recognition

#### 3. Language
- Vocabulary development
- Pre-reading skills
- Writing preparation
- Storytelling and conversation

#### 4. Mathematics
- Number recognition
- Counting and quantities
- Basic operations
- Pattern recognition

#### 5. Cultural
- Geography and cultures
- Science exploration
- Music and art
- History and time

### Reading Progress Reports

#### Observation Cards
Each observation includes:
- **Date and Time**: When it happened
- **Area**: Which Montessori area
- **Activity**: What the child was doing
- **Description**: Teacher's notes
- **Skills**: Specific skills demonstrated
- **Photos**: Visual documentation (with consent)

#### Progress Indicators
- 🌱 **Beginning**: First attempts
- 🌿 **Developing**: Making progress
- 🌳 **Mastering**: Confidently performing
- 🎯 **Extended**: Applying skill in new ways

### What to Look For
1. **Consistency**: Regular engagement in activities
2. **Growth**: Improvement over time
3. **Interests**: Areas child naturally gravitates toward
4. **Challenges**: Areas needing support
5. **Social Development**: Interactions with peers

## 💬 Communicating with Teachers

### Best Practices for Communication

#### When to Message Teachers
- Quick questions about your child's day
- Schedule changes (late arrival, early pickup)
- Health updates (allergies, illnesses)
- Home observations to share
- Appointment requests

#### When to Schedule a Meeting
- Progress concerns
- Behavioral questions
- Major transitions
- Annual reviews
- Special needs discussions

### Message Etiquette
1. **Be Specific**: "How was Thabo's concentration today?" not "How was Thabo?"
2. **Be Timely**: Message during school hours for quick response
3. **Be Respectful**: Teachers have many children to care for
4. **Use Photos**: Share home activities with photos
5. **Follow Up**: Thank teachers for their responses

### Emergency Communication
- Use phone call for urgent matters
- School will use SMS for critical alerts
- Check app for school closure notices
- Emergency contact information must be current

## 🏡 Supporting Montessori at Home

### Creating a Montessori-Friendly Home

#### Environment Setup
- **Order**: Everything has a place
- **Accessibility**: Child can reach what they need
- **Simplicity**: Fewer, better quality materials
- **Nature**: Incorporate natural elements
- **Beauty**: Pleasant, calm environment

#### Daily Routine
- **Consistency**: Regular schedule
- **Independence**: Allow child to do things themselves
- **Choice**: Offer limited, appropriate choices
- **Observation**: Watch without interrupting
- **Participation**: Include child in household tasks

### Activity Ideas by Age

#### 2-3 Years
- Pouring water between cups
- Sorting objects by color
- Matching socks
- Wiping surfaces
- Carrying small objects

#### 3-6 Years
- Setting table
- Preparing simple snacks
- Gardening activities
- Sorting laundry
- Basic cleaning tasks

#### 6-9 Years
- Meal planning assistance
- Basic cooking
- Pet care
- Organizing spaces
- Money management basics

### Connecting Home & School

#### Share Home Observations
1. Notice what interests your child
2. Take photos of activities
3. Write brief notes
4. Share with teacher via app
5. Discuss during conferences

#### Support Classroom Learning
1. Ask teacher for activity suggestions
2. Use similar language at home
3. Extend classroom projects
4. Read recommended books
5. Practice skills in real context

## 🔔 Notifications & Reminders

### Types of Notifications

#### Immediate (Push)

### Managing Notifications

## 🆘 Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Login Problems
```
Problem: Can't remember password
Solution: Use "Forgot Password" link

Problem: Account locked
Solution: Contact school administrator

Problem: App not loading
Solution: Clear browser cache or reinstall PWA
```

### Notification Issues
```
Problem: Not receiving notifications
Solution: Check app permissions on phone

Problem: Too many notifications
Solution: Customize notification settings

Problem: Notifications at wrong time
Solution: Set quiet hours in settings
```

### Sync Problems
```
Problem: Data not updating
Solution: Check internet connection

Problem: Photos not loading
Solution: Wait for better connection or use Wi-Fi

Problem: Messages not sending
Solution: App works offline - will send when online
```
